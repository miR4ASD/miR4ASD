miR4ASD: A Database of microRNAs associated in Autism Spectrum Disorder

Database Overview

miR4ASD is an open-access, literature-based database that compiles human microRNAs (miRNAs) that were experimentally associated with Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD). It includes manually curated data from peer-reviewed case-control studies, covering gene expression and genetic variant analysis. All miRNA names from different studies are standardized to miRBase version 22.1.

Key Features of miR4ASD:

Integrated Data
Integrates gene expression and miRNA profiling data from ASD-control studies.
Validated Target Genes
Incorporates DIANA-TarBase v9.0 strong experimental targets cross-referenced with SFARI Gene ASD scores.
Tissue-Specific Patterns
Highlights tissue-specific expression patterns across brain and peripheral samples.
Genetic Variants
Includes miRNAs with reported genetic variants — an underexplored area in ASD research.
Research Enabler
Supports biomarker discovery, functional studies, and genetic research in ASD.

Tissue Biological specimen or anatomical brain region profiled in the primary research. Blood, Brain, LCLs (Lymphoblastoid Cell Lines), Saliva, Serum, Plasma, Post-mortem brain (Cerebellum, Cortex, Temporal cortex, Frontal cortex, Superior temporal gyrus, Vermis), Dental pulp stem cells, Umbilical cord blood, Olfactory mucosal cells, Whole blood.
Overall evidence Synthesis metric reflecting consistency across all independent published studies for this miRNA.
  • Consistent upregulation: ≥2 independent studies all reporting increased expression.
  • Consistent downregulation: ≥2 independent studies all reporting decreased expression.
  • Conflicting evidence: ≥2 independent studies with opposing results (both up- and downregulated reported).
  • Single-study evidence: Supported by exactly 1 published study to date.
Number of studies (Upregulated / Downregulated) Count of independent peer-reviewed studies reporting upregulation or downregulation respectively. Non-negative integers (e.g. 0, 1, 2, 3+)
Total studies Sum of all independent expression studies evaluating this miRNA in ASD. Positive integer (≥1)

Precursor miRNA Corresponding genomic stem-loop hairpins encoding the targeting mature miRNA. Semicolon-separated miRBase precursor IDs.
Mature miRNA Mature microRNA guiding target mRNA recognition (links to miRBase). Standardized to miRBase v22.1.
Target Gene Official HGNC Gene Symbol of the validated mRNA target (links to GeneCards). e.g. PTEN, SHANK3, MECP2, CHD8, AGO1, AGO4
Gene Description Full descriptive name of the target protein-coding gene. e.g. phosphatase and tensin homolog, SH3 and multiple ankyrin repeat domains 3
ASD Susceptibility (SFARI) Curated autism risk score from the SFARI Gene database (Simons Foundation).
  • SFARI Cat 1 (High Confidence): Rigorously validated risk genes with ≥3 de novo likely gene-disrupting mutations in ASD.
  • SFARI Cat 2 (Strong Candidate): Genes with 2 de novo LGD mutations or genome-wide significance.
  • SFARI Cat 3 (Suggestive): Genes with suggestive single-study evidence.
  • SFARI Syndromic: Genes associated with established ASD-penetrant genetic syndromes.
  • Other Target: Validated target genes not currently cataloged in SFARI.
Evidence Level Experimental evidence classification tier from DIANA-TarBase v9.0.
  • Strong Evidence: Direct low-throughput molecular assays (Luciferase Reporter, Western Blot, qPCR, Northern Blot, ELISA).
  • Direct Binding (CLIP/CLASH): High-throughput physical cross-linking and sequencing (HITS-CLIP, PAR-CLIP, CLASH, qCLASH, RIP-Seq, AGO-IP).
  • High-Throughput Expression: Whole-transcriptome profiling (RNA-Seq, Microarrays, sRNA-Seq).
Experimental Methods Exact laboratory techniques used to confirm the miRNA-target interaction. Semicolon-separated methods (e.g. Luciferase Reporter Assay, Western Blot, HITS-CLIP, qPCR).
Regulation Directional regulatory consequence on target mRNA / protein expression. Negative (Downregulation), Positive (Upregulation), or — (Binding assay only).
Tissue / Cell Source Biological tissue or cell line model used in the experiment. e.g. Brain, Blood, HEK293, HeLa, Primary Neurons, etc.
PubMed Reference PubMed Identifiers (PMIDs) linking directly to the primary research articles. Clickable PMID badges (e.g. 24312487, 20144220).
